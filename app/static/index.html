For example

  • Which of Li et al.'s four dynamic models performed best on the Taiwan stock index, and did it beat the martingale?
  • What are the short-run and long-run effects of geopolitical risk on oil price volatility in the ARDL study?
  • In the Noviantri-group papers, does a shorter forecast horizon always give lower error?

Sources

    Answers come only from the papers and can still be wrong. Check the cited page.

    How it works

    Your question is matched against 647 passages from the papers using keyword search and meaning-based search together. The best passages, their neighbouring text and any tables they refer to go to Claude (Haiku 4.5), which must answer only from them and cite each sentence, or say the papers don't cover it. On a 66-question test set, the passage containing the answer reaches the model for 92% of questions, and 70% when questions are reworded in everyday language.
