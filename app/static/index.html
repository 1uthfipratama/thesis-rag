thesis-rag

Chat about 19 papers on ODE stock models, volatility and geopolitical risk.

Enter to send · Shift+Enter for a new line

For example

  • Explain like I'm 12: what does the logistic growth model say about the IDX Composite?
  • Which of Li et al.'s four dynamic models performed best on the Taiwan stock index, and did it beat the martingale?
  • What are the short-run and long-run effects of geopolitical risk on oil price volatility in the ARDL study?

Then ask follow-ups: “why?”, “say that more simply”, “what about the other papers?”

Claims about the papers are cited; general explanations are marked as such. Answers can still be wrong, so check the cited page.

How it works

Each message is matched against 647 passages from the papers using keyword search and meaning-based search together. Follow-ups like “what about the second one?” are first rewritten into a full question so the search knows what “it” means; “say that more simply” reuses the passages already found. The best passages, their neighbouring text and any tables they refer to go to Claude (Haiku 4.5) with the recent conversation. Anything it says about a paper must come from those passages and be cited; it may add plainly-labelled general background to explain. On a 66-question test set, the passage containing the answer reaches the model for 92% of questions, and 70% when questions are reworded in everyday language.

The conversation is kept in this browser tab only and is gone when you close it.
